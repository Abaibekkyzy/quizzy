Welcome to Quizzy!

Quizzy is a simple and fun platform for creating and playing interactive quizzes.

Whether you are a teacher, a student or just want to challenge your friends, Quizzy helps you build a quiz in a few clicks and play it anywhere.

What can you do with Quizzy?

📝
Create quizzes

Make your own quizzes with any number of questions and answer options.

🎮
Play with friends

Join a quiz and compete with other players in real time.

🏆
Track results

See your score right after the quiz and follow your progress.

📚
Manage your quizzes

Edit, update and delete your quizzes in the My Quizzes section.

🔒
Personal profile

Sign up and keep all your quizzes and results in one place.

📱
Works everywhere

Quizzy looks good on a computer, tablet and phone.
